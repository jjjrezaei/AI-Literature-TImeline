EX MACHINA 2014
Watching and reading the film “Ex Machina”, the film parallels the AI literature that we have read in class, “The Island of Doctor Moreau.” Both book and film show us humans attempt of playing against god through the creation of artificial life, and trying to gain control over it. Whether it be a human robot that was made by a human or a human discovering a humanlike creation made by god, both are discoveries of nonhuman artificial life. Their desire for control exposes the ethical dangers of dehumanization and how both creators run away from the thing they tried so hard to gain control over. The danger of losing empathy for something you have discovered, something these two scientists wanted to come across, and when they did, they lost empathy. This leads to losing control and having the artificial life having control over the maker. We lose empathy when fear is involved. When reading the film, we see certain tropes that are portrayed in the book “The Island of Doctor Moreau.” a scientist going to a off grid estate or island, coming across a creation and figuring out how to gain control over it. In “Ex Machina” a scientist named Nathan has his estate thats off grid to work on something big, a human robot that mimics a real human that this scientist made. Just like how Moreau is on offgrid island and finds creatures, the beat people. Both Nathan and Moreau cut themselves off from society to focus on their big projects, making nonhumans human. Both stories showcase power and control, Nathan through confinement and Moreau by pushing the “law of men” to the ape man, "It is a man. He must learn the law... not to go on all-fours, that is the law."(Wells 58) and "Men should not chase men, this is the law. Are we not men?"(Wells 59). Both Nathan and Moreau lack empathy for these creations, which is where the danger of dehumaizaion comes in, losing empthy allows the creations to gain control over the maker. Both of these stories show how we can’t control the creations but work with it, to appaoach the creation with empathy and patience. Creations can not be controlled just because you made it or found it. Confinement and implementing laws will only make the maker a threat to the creator which allows the creation agceny to gain control over the maker, which is the makers biggest fear. Both stories show how control collapses when you lack empathy. Both Nathan and Dr. Moreau show hoe science isnt evil but can be dangerous when you lack compassion and empthany, lacking both of those these because bother makers think because these creations are nonhuman they have to be controlled. The threat isnt the creations, it the creators.
