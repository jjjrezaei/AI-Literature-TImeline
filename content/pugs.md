# Pugs

The history of the dog breed known as Pugs starts in 400 B.C., where the breed originated in China. They were selectively bred in the hopes their wrinkled faces would resemble 王, the Chinese character for prince. From there, they traveled across Europe. Landing in the homes of the upper class, along with many royals, including Queen Victoria of England and Prince William of Holland. While they started as a royal breed, they have now become a beloved part of our culture,  even winning the Best in Show title at the 2024 National Dog Show. Pugs are praised for being loyal pets, with a temperament that is widely renowned for being intuitive and playful. Despite the adoration for these adorable animals, many people avoid adopting them due to the plethora of health problems that come with owning a pug. Unsurprisingly, pugs face many health issues in their face due to their shortened snout, which is called brachycephaly. These issues are due to the deformation of the upper airway and can include noisy and labored breathing, issues with the gag reflex, laryngeal collapse, and complications undergoing procedures involving anesthesia. Brachycephaly can also cause issues with the eyes, including dry eyes, increased risk of corneal scratches, inflammation of the cornea, and corneal pigmentation. Pugs are also three times more likely to become obese, likely due to breathing issues, making routine walks difficult. Their life expectancy is around 7 years, 4 years lower than the average dog life expectancy, which currently sits at around 11 years. They are frequently inbred and have difficulty giving birth due to their head shape. All of these health problems counteract the breed's companionship and good temperament, often leading people to shy away from adopting a pug into their family. Despite having no direct impact on the creation of AI, the creation and evolution of the pug, both physically and in the way we view them, is a prime example of how we, as humankind, treat our creations. The desire to create something new, while focusing only on aesthetics, was the driving force behind the creation of the pug breed as we know it today. The unintended consequences of this are the dramatic health problems for the dogs that drive away potential owners and create a difficult existence for the dogs themselves. Pugs show how humanity’s emphasis on aesthetics leads to design flaws that later result in a disregard for the creation, something that will happen eventually with AI if we are not careful.
 AI has been created with aesthetics in mind, and pugs demonstrate how humans create with an emphasis on aesthetics, which produces issues for the creation that eventually lead to its disregard. Much like the monster in Shelley’s Frankenstein, both pugs and AI are the result of the treatment of their creators. Pugs were created to have specific colorings and shapes in their wrinkles, but the unintended health consequences have made them undesirable. Frankenstein’s monster was created because Victor Frankenstein wanted to see if he could create life, with bodily aspects chosen specifically for beauty, but he abandoned his creation because of its ugliness, and the unintended consequences were the deaths of many of Victor’s loved ones. In both these cases, we have seen the negative consequences of creating while only caring about aesthetics, then abandoning the creation when a flaw is exposed. We have not reached that point with AI, and these two examples should provide a cautionary tale for how we develop and treat AI.  Pugs were our creation, our baby, and we now scorn them for something we made them to be.
