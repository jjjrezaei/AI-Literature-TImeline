# The Terminator

The Terminator  directed by James Cameron in 1984 has become one of the most influential pieces of popular culture, that depicts an apocalypse instigated entirely by AI.  After this films release, Skynet became the ubiguitous technological boogeyman alongside Hal from 2001: A Space Odyssey.  The cultural impact of the Terminator carried on into the present day where it is still referenced by the media, film, and even politicians trying to regulate AI. While the film did not influence actual AI production, its influence on popular culture and in the sphere of popular influence is undeniable.
